Privacy Policy

Effective date: April 29, 2026

Groce (“we”, “us”, or “our”) is a collaborative grocery list app for Android and iOS. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you use Groce, how we use it, and with whom we share it. We have tried to write this in plain language — if anything is unclear, please contact us at jordan@araujojordan.com.

1. What Information We Collect

Account information

When you create an account — either with an email and password or via Google Sign-In — we store:

  • Your email address
  • Your display name
  • Your profile photo URL (if provided by Google; otherwise none)

This information is stored in Firebase Authentication and in a users document in Cloud Firestore (Google Cloud project groce-3c697).

Grocery list data

When you use Groce, we store the content you create in Cloud Firestore:

  • List names and the email of the list owner
  • Item names, quantities, units, categories, and checked/unchecked state
  • Optional item photos you capture or select from your photo library
  • The display name of whoever added each item

Sharing and collaboration data

When you share a list, we store the email addresses of people you invite, people who have accepted an invitation, and people who have requested access via a shared link. This is necessary to grant the correct collaborators access to each list.

2. What We Do Not Collect

  • Your contacts or address book
  • Your location (we never request location permission)
  • Keystroke or usage analytics beyond what Firebase provides by default
  • Any data from people who have not created a Groce account

3. Third-Party Services

Groce integrates with several third-party services. When you use certain features, your data is sent to these services:

Google Firebase (Authentication & Firestore)

All user accounts and grocery data are stored in Google Firebase. Your data is processed according to Google’s Firebase Privacy Policy.

Google Sign-In

If you sign in with Google, we receive your Google account’s email address, name, and profile photo URL. We do not receive any other Google account data (e.g., contacts, Drive, Gmail).

OpenFoodFacts

When you scan a product barcode, the barcode number is sent to Open Food Facts to retrieve the product name and image. No account data is sent.

Image search services

When Groce fetches an image for a grocery item, it sends the item’s name as a search query to one or more of the following services: DuckDuckGo, Google Images, Bing Images, Brave Search, Yandex Images, and Startpage. Only the item name is sent — no account information or list content. Results are cached on your device to minimise repeat requests.

Unsplash

Groce may query the Unsplash API for product photos using item names as search terms. Unsplash’s own privacy policy governs how it handles those requests.

Google AdMob

Groce includes Google AdMob to display ads. AdMob may collect:

  • Your advertising identifier (Google Advertising ID on Android; IDFA on iOS)
  • Device information (model, OS version, language, time zone)
  • IP address and coarse location derived from IP

Facebook SDK (Android only)

The Android version of Groce includes the Facebook SDK. Facebook may collect device and app usage information in accordance with Meta’s Data Policy.

4. Device Permissions

Groce requests the following device permissions and uses them only for the stated purpose:

  • Camera — to scan product barcodes and to capture photos for grocery items.
  • Photo library — to let you attach an existing photo to a grocery item.
  • Internet — required for all features (Firebase sync, image search, etc.).

We do not access the camera or photo library for any other purpose.

5. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and authenticate your account.
  • To store, sync, and display your grocery lists to you and your collaborators.
  • To enable sharing features (invitations, join links, pending requests).
  • To fetch product information and images on your behalf.
  • To display advertisements via AdMob.

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

6. Sharing Your Data with Others

When you share a grocery list with another person, that person will be able to see:

  • All items in the list, including who added each item.
  • The display names and email addresses of other collaborators on the same list.

Only people you explicitly invite (or who you approve after they request access) can see a shared list.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Your grocery lists and items remain in Firestore until you delete them within the app. If you would like to delete your account and all associated data, please contact us at jordan@araujojordan.com and we will process your request within 30 days.

Note: in-app account self-deletion is not yet available. We plan to add it in a future update.

8. Children

Groce is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such information, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

9. Security

All data transmitted between the app and Firebase is encrypted in transit using TLS. Data stored in Firestore is encrypted at rest by Google. We apply Firebase security rules to ensure users can only read and write data they are authorised to access.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the effective date at the top of this page. Continued use of Groce after a change constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.

Questions or requests?
Contact us at jordan@araujojordan.com. We aim to respond within 5 business days.
