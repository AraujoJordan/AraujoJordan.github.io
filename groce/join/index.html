What is Groce?

Groce is a collaborative grocery list app for Android and iOS. Create lists, share them with family or friends, and shop together in real time.

  • Shared lists that sync instantly
  • Invite family & friends to collaborate
  • Scan product barcodes to add items instantly
  • Automatic product images from the web
  • Check off items as you shop
